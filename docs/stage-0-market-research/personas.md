# Bookora — Customer Personas

**Stage:** 0 · **Status:** Build complete · **Date:** 2026-06-05
Eight personas. Each: profile · pain points · goals · budget · buying triggers · decision factors · objections.
Budgets framed Africa-first (NGN/USD), with global note where relevant.

---

## P1 — Medspa Owner ("Dr. Ada")
- **Profile:** Owns a Lagos aesthetics/medspa clinic; 4 staff; high-value treatments; runs Instagram + WordPress site.
- **Pain points:** No-shows on expensive slots; manual WhatsApp back-and-forth; deposit collection painful; intake/consent forms on paper.
- **Goals:** Cut no-shows, take deposits up front, automate reminders, look premium.
- **Budget:** Mid-high — will pay Business (~$25–39/mo equiv) if it reduces no-shows; ROI-driven.
- **Buying triggers:** A costly no-show; competitor with slick online booking; Instagram-to-booking friction.
- **Decision factors:** Deposits, WhatsApp reminders, intake forms, professional look, Paystack.
- **Objections:** "Will clients actually use online booking?" "Is my data/clients' health info safe?" → answer with NDPR/consent + DPIA + WhatsApp familiarity.

## P2 — Salon / Barber Owner ("Tunde")
- **Profile:** Salon/barbershop, 2–6 chairs, walk-ins + appointments; price-sensitive; mobile-first.
- **Pain points:** Double-bookings, phone-tag, idle chairs, no-shows, staff schedule chaos.
- **Goals:** Fill chairs, reduce no-shows, let clients self-book via WhatsApp/IG link.
- **Budget:** Low-mid — Starter (~$7–12/mo equiv); very price-sensitive, loves free tier.
- **Buying triggers:** Busy season; new stylist; competitor posting "book online" links.
- **Decision factors:** Free/cheap, fast mobile booking, per-staff scheduling, Paystack/transfer, WhatsApp.
- **Objections:** "Too complicated/expensive," "My clients just call." → answer with free tier, 15-min setup, WhatsApp confirmations they already trust.

## P3 — Clinic / Dental / Physio ("MediCare Clinic")
- **Profile:** Small private clinic; multiple practitioners + rooms; recurring/follow-up visits.
- **Pain points:** Complex multi-resource scheduling, follow-ups, reminders, intake, records continuity.
- **Goals:** Reduce admin load, structured scheduling, reminders, patient history.
- **Budget:** Mid-high — Business; values reliability over price.
- **Buying triggers:** Front-desk overwhelmed; expansion; compliance pressure.
- **Decision factors:** Multi-staff/room, recurring, intake, reminders, audit log, NDPR/GDPR.
- **Objections:** "Compliance/security?" "Can it handle our complexity?" → audit log, data protection, scheduling depth.

## P4 — Coach / Trainer ("FitWithJoy")
- **Profile:** Fitness/life/business coach; 1:1 + group classes; online + in-person; strong social presence.
- **Pain points:** Selling sessions/packages, group class capacity, time zones (global clients), payment collection.
- **Goals:** Sell sessions/packages, run classes, automate scheduling + payment, look professional.
- **Budget:** Low-mid to mid — Starter/Business; willing to pay for time saved + revenue.
- **Buying triggers:** Growing client list; launching a group program; Calendly per-seat cost annoyance.
- **Decision factors:** Group bookings, deposits/packages, Stripe + Paystack, Zoom links, timezone handling.
- **Objections:** "I already use Calendly." → ownership, payments built in, your brand, group classes, flat price.

## P5 — Consultant ("StratAdvisory")
- **Profile:** Independent/boutique consultant; paid discovery + advisory calls; global + local clients.
- **Pain points:** Scheduling friction, charging for calls, no-shows on free intros, branding.
- **Goals:** Frictionless paid bookings, professional branded flow, calendar sync.
- **Budget:** Mid — Starter/Business; values brand + integrations.
- **Buying triggers:** Tired of Calendly branding/cost; wants paid calls; rebrand.
- **Decision factors:** Paid bookings, calendar sync, branded/self-hosted, Stripe + Paystack.
- **Objections:** "Calendly is simpler." → self-hosted brand/data, paid-call workflow, no per-seat tax.

## P6 — Lawyer ("Counsel Chambers")
- **Profile:** Solo/small law firm; paid consultations; confidentiality-critical.
- **Pain points:** Booking paid consults, confidentiality, no-shows, professional image.
- **Goals:** Paid consultation booking, confidential intake, reminders, gravitas.
- **Budget:** Mid-high — Business; conservative but pays for trust/quality.
- **Buying triggers:** Time wasted on scheduling; wants to monetize consults; image upgrade.
- **Decision factors:** Confidentiality/audit, paid bookings + deposits, professional branding, NDPR/GDPR.
- **Objections:** "Is client data confidential?" "Looks too 'salon-y'?" → security/audit emphasis + professional theme.

## P7 — Accountant / Bookkeeper ("LedgerPro")
- **Profile:** Accounting/tax practice; seasonal peaks (tax season); recurring client meetings.
- **Pain points:** Seasonal scheduling crunch, recurring meetings, document/intake collection, reminders.
- **Goals:** Smooth seasonal booking, recurring slots, intake docs, fewer no-shows.
- **Budget:** Mid — Starter/Business; ROI in saved admin time.
- **Buying triggers:** Tax-season overload; recurring-client management pain.
- **Decision factors:** Recurring bookings, intake/file upload, reminders, calendar sync, invoices/receipts.
- **Objections:** "Worth it outside peak season?" → year-round recurring + reminders value.

## P8 — Agency / Reseller ("PixelWorks Agency")
- **Profile:** Web/marketing agency building sites for many local SMB clients (salons, clinics, coaches).
- **Pain points:** Per-client booking setup repetition, no white-label, juggling many client tools, recurring revenue gap.
- **Goals:** Standardize on one tool, white-label as their brand, rebill clients, recurring revenue.
- **Budget:** High — Agency (~$99–149/mo equiv); justified by client billings.
- **Buying triggers:** New client batch; wanting recurring revenue; client asks for local payments/WhatsApp.
- **Decision factors:** Multi-tenant console, white-label, rebilling, templates/bulk, affiliate/partner program, reliability.
- **Objections:** "Can I trust it across all clients?" "Margin?" → multi-tenant reliability, white-label, rebilling + affiliate margins.
