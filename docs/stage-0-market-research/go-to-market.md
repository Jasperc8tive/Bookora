# Bookora — Go-To-Market Strategy

**Stage:** 0 · **Status:** Build complete · **Date:** 2026-06-05
30-day · 90-day · 6-month · 12-month plans. Africa-first (Nigeria lead) → global.
> Timelines are GTM-phase plans; they run **alongside** the engineering stage-gates (1–8), not before the product exists. "Day 0" = MVP/beta ready.

---

## 1. GTM Thesis

Win the **uncontested wedge** (Paystack/Flutterwave/WhatsApp on WordPress) in Nigeria first, prove no-show-reduction ROI with real SMBs, build topical SEO authority and an affiliate/agency channel, then expand across Africa and to the global "Calendly/Amelia alternative" market.

## 2. Pre-Launch (before Day 0 — overlaps build Stages 1–6)

- Build **email waitlist** via lead magnets (#1, #13, #25) + "building in public" content.
- Recruit **10–20 design-partner SMBs** (salon, medspa, clinic, coach) for closed beta + testimonials.
- Stand up marketing site with **home + 8 vertical LPs + 6 comparison LPs + pricing** (publish comparisons only after R-06 verification).
- Seed **Cluster 2/3/6 articles** (payments, WhatsApp, comparisons) so SEO is indexing before launch.
- Line up **affiliate/agency** early adopters + payment/hosting partner conversations.

## 3. 30-Day Plan (Launch month)

**Goal:** Public launch of free Bookora on wp.org + paid tiers; first 100–300 active installs; first paying customers.

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | wp.org launch | Submit free plugin; launch announcement to waitlist; publish home + top vertical/comparison LPs |
| 2 | Activation | Onboard beta partners publicly (case studies); push setup tutorial videos (#1–8); WhatsApp/Paystack how-to articles |
| 3 | Channel ignition | Open affiliate program; recruit first creators; agency landing page live |
| 4 | Conversion | First upgrade campaigns (deposits/reminders ROI); collect reviews; iterate onboarding from real data |

**KPIs:** installs, TTFB <15min, first MRR, wp.org reviews ≥4.5★, first affiliate signups.

## 4. 90-Day Plan

**Goal:** Establish product-market-fit signals in Nigeria; ~1–3k active installs; repeatable acquisition.

- **Content engine** at ~8–10 articles + 4 videos/month (front-load wedge + comparison clusters).
- **Vertical pushes:** dedicated campaigns for salon, medspa, coach (highest-fit personas) with tailored LPs + lead magnets.
- **Community/BD:** webinars with vertical communities; Paystack/Flutterwave directory listings; African hosting partner talks.
- **Affiliate scale-up:** 20–50 active affiliates; first agency partners onboarded + rebilling.
- **Iterate:** prioritize Stage-build backlog from real usage (no-show reduction proof, WhatsApp reliability).

**KPIs:** free→paid 2–4%, activation ≥40%, NSM (paid bookings/mo) trending up, retention cohorts forming, agency pipeline.

## 5. 6-Month Plan

**Goal:** Category challenger in African WP booking; ~10k installs (BO-1 target); diversified channel mix; expand beyond Nigeria.

- **Geo expansion:** Ghana, Kenya, South Africa — localize payments (mobile money), geo LPs, local affiliates.
- **SEO maturity:** all 7 clusters live; comparison + vertical pages ranking; calculators earning backlinks.
- **Agency channel** producing meaningful MRR share; white-label tier live and selling.
- **Product depth** (per stage-gates): calendar sync, recurring, group, reporting fully shipped & marketed.
- **Partnerships:** formal listings/co-marketing with Paystack/Flutterwave + hosting.

**KPIs:** 10k installs, MRR milestone, agency-MRR climbing toward 25% (BO-3), retention ≥85% (paid), multi-country installs.

## 6. 12-Month Plan

**Goal:** Default booking plugin for African WP SMBs; credible global "Amelia/Calendly alternative"; durable revenue engine.

- **Global push:** lead with pillars 1/2/6/7 (speed, Elementor, modern UX, ownership) + Stripe/PayPal; target "Calendly/Amelia alternative" keywords in Western markets.
- **100 articles + 50 videos + 25 lead magnets** fully deployed (the content plan executed).
- **Channel maturity:** affiliate ≥20% of installs, agency ≥25% of MRR.
- **Enterprise/white-label** resellers onboarded; partner/reseller directory.
- **Brand:** "the African booking platform that went global" narrative; conference/community presence.

**KPIs:** ARR milestone, LTV:CAC ≥3:1, payback <12mo, multi-region revenue, top-3 SERP for wedge keywords, low churn.

## 7. Channel Mix & Budget Logic (Africa-first reality)

| Channel | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| wp.org free tier | #1 | Zero-CAC flywheel |
| SEO (wedge + comparison + vertical) | #1 | Compounding, owns empty keyword space |
| Affiliate / creators | #2 | Low CAC, vertical reach |
| Agency / reseller | #2 | High LTV multiplier |
| Partnerships (Paystack/Flutterwave/hosting) | #2 | Trust + distribution |
| WhatsApp/IG organic + Shorts | #3 | Matches target-market behavior |
| Paid ads | #4 (later) | Only once unit economics proven; retargeting first |

**Rationale:** paid ads are CAC-heavy and risky in a price-sensitive market; lead with owned + earned + channel before paid.

## 8. Risks to GTM

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Product not ready when marketing peaks | GTM phases pegged to Day 0 = MVP-ready, not calendar |
| Comparison pages factually stale (R-06) | Verify + date-stamp + maintain |
| WhatsApp onboarding friction blunts wedge (R-02) | Guided setup + optional managed relay |
| "Africa-only" perception caps global (R-07) | Global-competitive pillars + explicit global tier from launch |
| Channel concentration (over-reliant on wp.org SEO) | Diversify into affiliate + agency + partnerships early |

→ Validated against the Stage 0 audit ([stage-audit.md](stage-audit.md)).
