## 5. Internal Linking Strategy

- **Hub-and-spoke:** each cluster article links **up** to its pillar and **sideways** to 2–3 sibling articles; pillar links **down** to all spokes.
- **Money pages get link equity:** comparison + vertical + pricing pages receive contextual links from every relevant article ("Bookora vs Bookly", "salon booking").
- **Wedge cross-links:** every payments/WhatsApp article cross-links to the relevant vertical page (medspa→deposits, salon→WhatsApp).
- **Anchor hygiene:** descriptive, varied anchors; avoid over-optimization.
- **CTA blocks:** contextual "Try Bookora free" + lead-magnet offers in-content, not just header.

## 7. Comparison Page Strategy

- One page per competitor: **"Bookora vs [X]"** + **"Best [X] Alternative"** variant.
- Honest, specific, table-driven (reuse [competitor-research.md](competitor-research.md) matrix) — **lead with the wedge** (local rails + WhatsApp + bundled value) without trashing competitors (credibility + avoids legal issues; respects trademark — D-001).
- Include "When to choose [X] instead" for trust.
- ⚠️ **Gate on R-06:** verify competitor facts/pricing before publishing; keep updated.
- Schema: comparison/FAQ structured data for SERP features.

- Programmatic care for vertical×geo combos (avoid thin/duplicate pages).
